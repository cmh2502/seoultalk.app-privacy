Delete Your SeoulTalk Account

This page explains how users of the SeoulTalk app can delete their account and the data associated with it.

1. Delete your account in the app

  1. Open the SeoulTalk app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Me tab and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm to permanently delete your account.

2. Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to minhee.choi@hyperzen.co.kr from the email address registered to your account, with the subject "Delete my SeoulTalk account". We will delete your account and associated data within 30 days and notify you by email.

3. Data that is deleted

  • Account information: email address, user name, country, and profile photo
  • Posts, comments, and images you uploaded
  • Chat messages and voice call records
  • Push notification tokens linked to your account

4. Data that may be retained

  • Chat messages and voice call records are automatically deleted within 30 days, even before account deletion.
  • Server logs may be kept for up to 30 days for security purposes and are then deleted.
  • Information we are required to keep by law may be retained for the period required by law.

For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
